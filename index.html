Privacy Policy

Application: Zodd Von Gotz Content Publisher

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. About the Application

Zodd Von Gotz Content Publisher is a private tool developed to help its operator prepare and publish original print-on-demand design content to an authorized Pinterest account.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized, the application may access Pinterest account information, boards, and Pins necessary to manage content publishing.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to select boards, create Pins, manage published content, and link Pins to relevant TeePublic product pages.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored privately and are not published in public source repositories.

5. Data Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or provide account data to advertisers. Information necessary to publish Pins is transmitted to Pinterest through its official API.

6. Access and Revocation

The account owner may revoke the application's access through the available Pinterest account authorization settings.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application's features and data practices change.
