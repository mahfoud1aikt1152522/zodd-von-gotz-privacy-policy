Privacy Policy

Application: Zodd Von Gotz Content Publisher

Operator: Zodd Von Gotz

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. About the Application

Zodd Von Gotz Content Publisher is a private application developed to help its operator prepare, organize, and publish original print-on-demand design content to an authorized Pinterest account.

2. Information Accessed

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access Pinterest account information, boards, and Pins necessary to manage content publishing.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to select Pinterest boards, create and manage Pins, organize publishing workflows, and link Pins to relevant TeePublic product pages.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored privately and are not published in public source repositories. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information used by the application.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or provide Pinterest account data to advertisers. Information required to publish Pins is transmitted to Pinterest through its official API.

6. Access and Revocation

The Pinterest account owner may revoke the application's access through the available Pinterest account authorization settings.

Privacy-related questions and requests may also be sent to the contact email listed below.
